# · The Fedora counterpart of pacman.txt: `./setup packages` installs
# · whatever is missing with `dnf install`. `#` starts a comment anywhere on
# · a line, a commented-out line is skipped, and a line starting with `@` is
# · a DNF group. The first two sections are required; each later one is a
# · feature that disables itself when its packages are missing.
#
# · Fedora's repositories do not carry Hyprland itself, awww, the Hyprland
# · tools, or several of the smaller things: those are answered for in
# · fedora-third-party.txt, which `./setup packages` offers after this one.
# · Nothing outside Fedora's own repositories is installed from here.
